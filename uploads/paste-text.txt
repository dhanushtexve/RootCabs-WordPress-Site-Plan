Yes, I reviewed your SEO strategy presentation. It is a very good start and the analysis is directionally correct. The biggest recommendation I'd add is **not just creating service pages, but building a complete city ecosystem**. 

## 1. City Pages should become a major navigation item

Instead of only having service pages, create a dedicated **"Cities"** section.

Example:

```
Cities

Tamil Nadu
 ├── Chennai
 ├── Vellore
 ├── Ranipet
 ├── Tiruvannamalai
 ├── Kanchipuram
 ├── Arcot
 ├── Katpadi
 ├── Ambur
 ├── Gudiyatham
 ├── Hosur
 ├── Salem
 ├── Coimbatore
 ├── Trichy
 ├── Madurai
 ├── Erode
 ├── Tiruppur
 ├── Pondicherry
```

Every city should have its own landing page.

Example:

```
rootcabs.com/chennai
rootcabs.com/vellore
rootcabs.com/coimbatore
```

Each page should have:

* Hero section
* Available services
* Popular destinations
* Fare estimate
* Local attractions
* FAQ
* Customer reviews
* Driver registration CTA
* App download

This gives Google and AI systems much richer geographic coverage.

# 3. Add Route Pages

This is one of the biggest SEO opportunities.

Examples

```
Vellore → Chennai

Vellore → Bangalore

Chennai → Pondicherry

Chennai → Tirupati

Coimbatore → Ooty

Madurai → Rameswaram
```

URL

```
/vellore-to-chennai-taxi

/chennai-to-vellore-taxi
```

These have high commercial intent.

## Overall assessment

I would rate the presentation **9.5/10**. It identifies the right competitive gaps—dedicated service pages, location pages, structured data, AI-friendly content, and a 90-day roadmap. 

To make it a **10/10 enterprise SEO strategy**, I'd add:

* A statewide **Cities Hub** with a scalable page hierarchy.
* District and landmark landing pages.
* Route-specific pages for high-intent travel.
* Driver-focused SEO pages.
* Dynamic fare estimation tools.
* Strong internal linking between cities, services, landmarks, and blogs.
* A reusable content template so every new city page follows the same optimized structure.

That architecture would give Root Cabs a much stronger foundation to scale organically across Tamil Nadu rather than optimizing one city at a time.
